# CareLink Product Scope

## Roles

- Patient
- Doctor
- Nurse
- Hospital/Clinic
- Admin

## MVP

### Patient
Registration, profile, AI-assisted symptom intake, care pathway, provider/hospital discovery, appointments, home-care requests, secure messaging, notifications and follow-up.

### Provider
Registration, credential verification, professional profile, availability, consultations, home visits, secure messaging and AI-generated patient intake summaries.

### Hospital
Facility profile, services, departments, professionals, referrals and appointments.

### Admin
User/provider/hospital verification, service management, appointments, home visits, payments, complaints, analytics, safety events and audit logs.

## AI Boundaries

The AI collects information, asks adaptive questions, identifies configured safety signals, explains care options, prepares clinician handoff summaries and assists with matching. Clinical diagnosis and final treatment decisions remain with qualified healthcare professionals.

## Later

Pharmacy, laboratory integrations, ambulance coordination, insurance, wearables and advanced clinical integrations are intentionally deferred until the core MVP is stable.
